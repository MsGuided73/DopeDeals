# 🌿 Highway 420: Staff Admin Guide
*The complete non-technical manual for managing the Highway 420 storefront.*

---

## 🔑 **How to Access the Admin Panel**

1.  **Open your browser** and go to: `https://highway420store.com/admin` (or your specific store URL).
2.  **Log in** using your staff email and password.
3.  **The Dashboard:** Once logged in, you will see the main Dashboard, which gives you a high-level summary of today's sales, active customers, and any items that are low on stock.

---

## 📊 **The Main Sections**

The sidebar on the left is your primary navigation. Here is what each section does:

### **1. Store Management**
*   **Dashboard:** Your "at-a-glance" view of the store's performance.
*   **Products:** Add new items to the shop, change prices, or update product descriptions and images.
*   **Categories:** Group your products (e.g., "Bongs", "Vapes") to make them easier for customers to find.
*   **Inventory:** Check exactly how many of each item you have left. This section will show you **Low Stock Alerts** so you know when to reorder.

### **2. Sales & Customers**
*   **Orders:** Your most important daily tool. See every purchase made, print receipts, and update shipping statuses.
*   **Customers:** View your customer database, check who your "VIP" shoppers are, and see their order history.
*   **Shipping:** Manage your warehouse locations and ShipStation settings.
*   **Payments:** Reconcile sales and monitor the health of your KajaPay payment processor.

### **3. Content & Visibility**
*   **Assets:** Manage all the photos and media used across the site.
*   **SEO:** Simple tools to help your products show up higher in Google search results.
*   **Page Builder:** A visual tool to change the look and feel of your homepage or create new landing pages.

### **4. Advanced Tools**
*   **AI Tools:** Access our built-in AI that automatically classifies products and suggests descriptions.
*   **Compliance:** **CRITICAL.** Monitor age verifications to ensure all customers are legally allowed to purchase.
*   **Integrations:** Check the "Health" of your connections to Zoho, ShipStation, and other apps.
*   **Design:** Adjust the visual appearance of the home page, including colors and fonts.

## 🛠️ **Common Daily Tasks**

### **✅ Processing New Orders**
1.  Go to **Orders** in the sidebar.
2.  Look for orders marked as **"Pending"**.
3.  Click **"View Details"** to see what the customer bought.
4.  Once the items are packed, change the status to **"Processing"** or **"Shipped"**.
5.  If using ShipStation, your tracking numbers will sync automatically!

### **📦 Adding a New Product**
1.  Go to **Products** and click the green **"Add Product"** button.
2.  Enter the **Name**, **Price**, and **SKU**.
3.  Upload **Images** by clicking the camera icon.
4.  Set the **Initial Stock** so the site knows how many are available.
5.  Click **"Save"** to make it live on the site.

### **🔒 Checking Age Verifications**
1.  Go to **Compliance** in the sidebar.
2.  Click the **"Age Verifications"** tab.
3.  Any customers waiting for approval will appear here.
4.  Review their ID (if provided) and click **"Approve"** to grant them access to the store.

### **🎨 Customizing Site Design (Scrolling Bar)**
1.  Go to **Design** in the sidebar.
2.  **To Change Colors:** Use the color pickers in the "Visual Identity" section to update the background or text of the scrolling brand bar.
3.  **To Adjust Height:** Use the "Section Height" slider to make the bar taller or shorter on the home page.
4.  **To Change Fonts:** Use the "Typography" dropdowns to select a different look for your headings.
5.  **Preview:** Check the **Live Preview** on the right side of the screen to see how your changes will look on a mobile phone before publishing.
6.  **Publish:** Click the orange **"Publish Changes"** button to make the new design live for all customers.

### **📰 Managing the Site Blog**
1.  Go to **Assets** or your dedicated **Blog** section (if visible).
2.  **To Create a Post:** Click the black **"New Post"** button at the top right.
3.  **Fill in the details:**
    *   **Title:** The headline of your article.
    *   **Excerpt:** A short summary (1-2 sentences) that appears on the blog listing page.
    *   **Content:** The main body of your post. You can use standard text or even simple HTML for formatting.
    *   **Category:** Choose a relevant category like "Education" or "Culture".
    *   **Image URL:** Paste a link to the main image you want to use for the post.
4.  **Featured:** Check the "Mark as featured" box if you want this post to appear in special sections of the homepage.
5.  **Save:** Click **"Create Post"** to publish it to the site.

## 🆘 **Troubleshooting & Help**

### **Is something not working?**
*   **Check Integrations:** Go to the **Integrations** page. If you see a red "Error" or "Warning" next to Zoho or KajaPay, the connection might be down. Click **"Refresh Status"** to check again.
*   **Missing Products:** If a product isn't showing up, check its status in the **Products** page. Ensure it is marked as **"Active"**.

### **Who to Contact**
*   **Technical Issues:** Contact the development team via the dedicated Slack channel.
*   **Payment Issues:** Reach out to KajaPay Support at `support@kajapay.com`.
